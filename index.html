Safe to skip for now

  • Learning to code. You describe what you want in plain words, and you check the result.
  • Installing things on your laptop. Claude Code runs in the browser and in the Claude app, so I do most of my work from a phone.
  • Plugins, connectors and custom agents. They’re useful later. Get the basics in place first.
