About

I’m a senior data analyst at a large media company. SQL, BigQuery and machine learning are my day job.

Lately I build with AI agents almost every day: small, real tools that take everyday work off my plate, from my phone as much as from my laptop.

I like to work carefully. My agents follow a written rulebook, every change goes through a review that I approve, and a small guard script blocks the risky moves before they happen. When something goes wrong, I write down the mistake and the rule it taught me.

This site is where I share all of it, so you can learn the same things faster than I did. If you’d like to talk about AI, agents or anything here, find me on LinkedIn.
