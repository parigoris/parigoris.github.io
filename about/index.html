About

I’m a senior data analyst, and lately I can’t stop building with AI.

What excites me most is working with AI agents: giving them clear goals and good guardrails, checking what they make, and handing them the everyday jobs that eat up my time. I build something small and real almost every day.

This site is where I share what I find. Each lesson shows what I asked for, how it works in plain words, and what I learned on the way. If something here saves you a few hours or sparks an idea, it has done its job.

Away from the screen, I train Brazilian jiu-jitsu, a sport people often call human chess. It has taught me how real skills get built, slowly and one small step at a time. It has also given me a community, good friends and a regular dose of humility.
