About

By day I’m a senior data analyst at a large media company. The job has taught me to trust a number more than a promise, so I test my AI setup the way I’d test a model: I try to break it.

My agents work behind a small guard script that blocks risky moves, such as saving straight to my main branch. When I had a helper AI attack it, most of its attempts got past. I rebuilt the guard to say no whenever it isn’t sure, and chapter 3 has the before and after.

I also build things that do real work, mostly from my phone. One is a scheduled agent that looks for useful AI news every Monday and Thursday morning and reports back to me. It can search the web, but it isn’t allowed to change a file or push anything, because an agent that reads the internet shouldn’t also have the power to act. This site came together the same way, in one afternoon, with every change checked in phone screenshots before I merged it.

Here I write about building with AI agents for people who have something to build and no coding background, so you can skip the mistakes I’ve already made.

If you’re building something and want to compare notes, say hi on LinkedIn.
